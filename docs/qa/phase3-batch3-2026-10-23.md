## Necessary duplicate-check correction

The old check compared a body URL's `pathname` to the literal mapped `href`. New hrefs with `#paperwork-title` would therefore never match an existing body link. It now extracts the mapped URL's pathname before comparison. Parent-service or fragment links suppress an extra note; the rendered link still uses the exact approved fragment. Existing seven mappings have no fragment and retain their previous behavior and copy. No additional HTTP/Wix requests, imports, cache, fallback, retry or client JavaScript.
