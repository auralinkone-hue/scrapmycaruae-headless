### Query/performance boundaries

- **One query replaces one query per service request.** Previously: newest eight records. Now: `queryPosts({ fieldsets: ['SEO'] }).hasSome('slug', selectedSlugs).limit(3).find()`. Wix’s documented Posts filter table explicitly supports `slug` with `$hasSome`: https://dev.wix.com/docs/api-reference/business-solutions/blog/posts-stats/filter-and-sort .
- No extra per-article lookups, token-generation calls in the loader, pagination, complete-inventory queries, new cache or retry/fallback request. Wix SDK authentication behavior is unchanged.
- Requesting SEO allows intentionally noindexed guides to be omitted. Disabled SEO directives do not suppress a guide. Unrelated/malformed/duplicate records are excluded, and original Wix objects remain intact.
- Missing/unpublished/noindexed selected posts leave fewer cards; empty or failed optional retrieval hides the existing section. No newest-only unrelated fallback or placeholder content.
- **One read-only SDK verification** of the engine-service selection returned all three exact older protected slugs in relevance order. This verifies the real supported query, not a speedup or guaranteed future availability. Request-count and limit assertions are automated; no quantitative latency/CWV improvement is claimed.
- Existing `getRelevantPosts` users (valuation, flood, registration, old-car, fleet and removal pages), homepage/blog latest sections and article related/latest sidebars were inspected as applicable but are not changed by this focused newest-only batch.
